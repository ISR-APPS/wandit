The design

Made slowly, on purpose.

N° 01

Thrown, not molded

Each orb is thrown on the wheel in our Lisbon atelier, so the wall of every shade varies by a hair's breadth. That variance is the point — no two lamps diffuse light identically, and yours is numbered to prove it.

N° 02

2700K, nothing harsher

The integrated LED is tuned to candle-adjacent warmth — 2700 kelvin at CRI 95 — filtered twice through the ceramic body. It renders skin, wood and wine the way dusk does, not the way offices do.

N° 03

Dimmed like an instrument

The brass stem is the switch. Tap it to step through three levels; hold it and the glow sweeps smoothly from reading-bright to almost-ember. No app, no remote, nothing to lose.

The second colorway

After dark, Noir.

The same orb, glazed in deep charcoal and set on raw basalt. Unlit, it reads as sculpture. Lit, the glaze holds the glow inside the sphere — a lamp that keeps its light close, like a well-kept secret.
